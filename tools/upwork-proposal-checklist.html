    What “good enough” usually means

    • Opening names this job’s outcome or constraint (not a bio).
    • One relevant proof point, stated truthfully.
    • Clear includes/excludes if you mention price.
    • One useful question + a low-pressure next step.
    • Timezone as logistics, not an apology.
